S3  [T+0:20-0:30] Payroll maths

PROMPT:
Write rules/payroll.py using Decimal.
compute_tax_monthly(gross_monthly) -> Decimal: annual = gross_monthly * 12; slabs: 0..600000 => 0;
600000..1200000 => 5% of the part above 600000; 1200000..2400000 => 30000 + 15% of the part above 1200000;
2400000..3600000 => 210000 + 25% of the part above 2400000; above 3600000 => 510000 + 35% of the part above 3600000.
Monthly tax = annual tax / 12, rounded to 2 decimals.
compute_payslip(basic, join_date, exit_date, month, working_days_in_month, payable_working_days, absent_days, unpaid_days,
half_days) -> dict with keys basic, gross, working_days, absent_days, unpaid_days, half_days, deduction, tax, net.
gross = 1.5 * basic. per_day = gross / working_days_in_month. deduction = per_day * (absent_days + unpaid_days + 0.5*half_days).
Joiners/leavers: payable_working_days (days between join and exit inside the month) replaces the working days for proration:
prorated_gross = per_day * payable_working_days, and deduction uses the same per_day. Tax is computed on the full
(unprorated) monthly gross, as decided in PROJECT.md. net = prorated_gross - deduction - tax.
Tests: each slab boundary (annual 600k, 1.2M, 2.4M, 3.6M), a full-month employee with no absences, one with 2 absences and
1 half day, a mid-month joiner. Output only the files.
